## ⚠️ AVOID These Black Hat Tactics

❌ **Don't Do:**
- Buy backlinks (Google penalty)
- Keyword stuffing
- Hidden text/links
- Duplicate content
- Link farms/PBNs
- Auto-generated content without review
- Cloaking (showing different content to bots)

✅ **Do Instead:**
- Earn links through quality content
- Write naturally with keywords
- Transparent, user-friendly design
- Original, valuable content
- Quality directories only
- Human-written content
- Same content for users and bots
