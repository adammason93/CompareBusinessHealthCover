## 💡 Pro Tips for Keyword Success

1. **Focus on Search Intent**: Match content to what users actually want
2. **Use Natural Language**: Write for humans first, search engines second
3. **Target Long-Tail First**: Easier to rank, higher conversion rates
4. **Update Content Regularly**: Keep pages fresh and relevant
5. **Internal Linking**: Connect related keywords and topics
6. **Monitor Competitors**: Track what they rank for, find gaps
7. **Featured Snippets**: Format content for position zero opportunities
8. **Local SEO**: Don't ignore "near me" and local terms
9. **Mobile Optimization**: 60%+ of searches are mobile
10. **Track Everything**: You can't improve what you don't measure

## ⚠️ Keyword Cannibalization Prevention

Avoid targeting the same keyword on multiple pages:

**Problem Example:**

- Homepage: "health insurance UK"
- Health Insurance Guide: "health insurance UK"
- Family page: "health insurance UK"

**Solution:**

- Homepage: "health insurance UK", "compare health insurance"
- Guide: "what is health insurance", "how health insurance works"
- Family: "family health insurance UK", "family health cover"

**Rule:** One primary keyword per page, with related secondary keywords
