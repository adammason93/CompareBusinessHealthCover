## 📊 Backlink Tracking & Management

### Tools to Use

**Free Tools:**
- Google Search Console - Monitor existing backlinks
- Google Alerts - Track brand mentions
- Ahrefs Backlink Checker (Limited free) - Check backlink profile
- Ubersuggest - Basic backlink analysis

**Paid Tools (Recommended):**
- Ahrefs (£99/month) - Comprehensive backlink analysis
- SEMrush (£99/month) - Competitor backlink research
- Moz Pro (£79/month) - Domain authority tracking

### Tracking Spreadsheet

Create a spreadsheet with these columns:
1. Target Website
2. Domain Authority
3. Contact Name
4. Contact Email
5. Outreach Date
6. Follow-up Date
7. Status (Pending/Approved/Declined)
8. Link URL (if successful)
9. Link Type (Guest Post/Directory/Mention)
10. DoFollow/NoFollow
11. Notes

## ⚠️ Important Guidelines

### DO:
✅ Focus on relevance over quantity
✅ Build relationships, not just links
✅ Create genuinely helpful content
✅ Diversify your link sources
✅ Get links from UK-based sites when possible
✅ Monitor your backlink profile monthly
✅ Remove toxic/spammy backlinks
✅ Be patient - link building takes 3-6 months

### DON'T:
❌ Buy links from link farms
❌ Use automated link building tools
❌ Spam comments on blogs
❌ Create fake profiles for links
❌ Use exact match anchor text excessively
❌ Get links too quickly (appears unnatural)
❌ Ignore link relevance
❌ Forget to track your efforts

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Quality > Quantity**: One DA 70 link is worth 10 DA 30 links
2. **Relevance Matters**: Insurance/finance links are more valuable than random links
3. **Natural Anchor Text**: Vary your anchor text naturally
4. **Link Velocity**: Don't get 50 links in one week then nothing
5. **Relationship Building**: The best links come from genuine relationships
6. **Content First**: Create amazing content and links will follow
7. **Track Everything**: You can't improve what you don't measure
8. **Be Patient**: SEO and link building take 6-12 months to show results
